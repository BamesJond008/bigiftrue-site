Common questions

My match failed to score. Tap TRY AGAIN. Your recording is still on your phone. Several retries of the same recording are free.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel the Pass? Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then choose Big If True. Apple handles all billing, and refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data? Email us with the player names and roughly when you played, and we'll delete everything tied to your account. Deleting the app also removes the recordings kept on your phone.

Are the fact-checks always right? No. They're made by AI and can be wrong. Treat them as entertainment, not as authoritative fact.
